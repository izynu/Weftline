# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Everyone participating in this project, including the maintainers, is
expected to communicate with honesty, competence, and respect. We are building
a tool about careful work; conduct in the project should model the same.

Expected behavior:

- Critique work and ideas precisely; keep it about the artifact, not the
  person.
- Be honest about what you verified and what you assume. Fabricated results,
  test claims, or credentials are treated seriously.
- Respect that maintainers make final decisions on direction and releases.
- Keep discussions on topic, accessible, and free of harassment, insults, or
  discriminatory language of any kind.

Unacceptable behavior includes: personal attacks, discriminatory or demeaning
conduct, publishing others' private information without consent, bad-faith
participation, and misrepresenting authorship or contribution.

## Scope

This standard applies within project spaces (issues, pull requests,
discussions) and when a person represents the project publicly.

## Enforcement

The maintainer, Zynu (@izynu), is responsible for enforcement. Report
violations by opening a private GitHub issue marked as a conduct report or by
contacting the maintainer through GitHub. Reports will be handled with
reasonable confidentiality.

Consequences are proportionate: a private warning with an explanation, a
temporary or permanent ban from project spaces, or removal of contributions,
depending on severity and pattern. Fabricating verification results or
misattributing authorship in project materials is grounds for removal of the
content at minimum.

Maintainers are held to the same standard. A maintainer who violates it
should be reported the same way, and the report will be taken seriously.
